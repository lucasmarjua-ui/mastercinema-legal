Frequently asked questions

I think a question has the wrong answer.

Please email us the question and the answer you think is correct. We check every report and fix confirmed mistakes in the next update.

Can I play without an account?

Yes. Tap Play on the first screen and you play as a guest straight away. Your progress is saved, and if you create an account later (from your Profile, or when the results screen suggests it), everything comes with you. To appear on the leaderboards and be found by other players, you need an account.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, go to Settings → Delete account. You will be asked to confirm with your nickname, and with your password if your account has one. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address linked to your account.

How do I report or block another player?

Long-press one of their messages, or open their player card and choose Report or Block. Reporting also blocks the player. You can see and undo blocks in Settings.

Can I play on a new phone?

Yes, if you have an account: sign in with your nickname or email and your progress, collection and rank come with you. A guest account cannot be moved to another phone, so create an account first.

Does the game cost money?

No. MasterCinema is free to play and has no purchases with real money at the moment.
