Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information the MasterCinema movie trivia game ("the game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game is made by Lucas Martínez, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for your data (the "data controller"). If anything here is unclear, write to lucas.mar.jua@gmail.com.

What we collect

Playing as a guest

You can start playing without creating an account. When you do, the game creates an anonymous account with Firebase Authentication (Google): it has a random identifier and no email address or password. Your nickname and progress are saved to that anonymous account so they are not lost if the app closes. Guests do not have a public profile: they do not appear on leaderboards or in social features.

If you later create an account, your email and password are linked to that same anonymous account, so your progress carries over. If you never create one, your guest account stays tied to this installation of the game; you can delete it at any time in Settings → Delete account.

Account information

When you create an account we store your email address and the nickname you choose. So that you can sign in with either of them, we also store a one-way hash of your email address that lets the game look up your nickname; it does not reveal your email to other players. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google); we never see or store it ourselves.

Profile and game progress

To run the game we store your avatar, banner and other cosmetic choices, level, experience points, ranked position, in-game currency, quests, ticket collection, statistics and match results. Once you have an account, your nickname, avatar, banner, level and rank are visible to other players on leaderboards, in duels and in social features.

Social features

If you use them, we store your friends list, friend requests, groups, duels against other players and messages you send to other players. If you report a player, we store the report (who reported whom, the reason and the reported content) so we can review it. Players you block are stored only on your own device.

Usage analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand how the game is played, for example how many people come back the next day, how long sessions last and which modes are played. Firebase collects app usage events, device model, operating system, app version, approximate country (derived from your IP address) and a random app-instance identifier. We do not send your account ID, email, nickname or messages to analytics, and we do not use analytics to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Anonymous question statistics

To keep questions at the right difficulty, the game adds to shared counters of how often each question is shown and answered correctly. These counters contain only numbers per question; they do not record who answered.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. Profile pictures are chosen from the game's own avatars; you cannot upload a photo.
  • We do not show advertising of any kind and do not use the device advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • There are no purchases with real money: the in-game currencies are earned by playing.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

How we use it and our legal basis

We use your information to provide the game (your account, progress, matches, leaderboards and social features), which is necessary to perform our agreement with you. We use analytics, anonymous question statistics and player reports on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the game, balancing its difficulty and keeping it safe.

Who processes your data

The game is built on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Analytics), provided by Google. Google processes this data on our behalf and may store it on servers outside the United Kingdom, under the safeguards described in Google's data processing terms. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information and progress for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (including a guest account), your profile, nickname, friends, groups, open duels, public leaderboard entry and sign-in credentials are deleted, and your nickname is removed from past matches. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for a limited period and then deleted automatically. Anonymous question statistics are not linked to you and are kept.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the game: Settings → Delete account. Deletion is permanent. If you can no longer sign in, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to receive a copy of it. Email us and we will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also tell you inside the game.
